Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026.

Octo Magic is a game. This page explains what data it processes when you play online.

Data collected

  • Account: login goes through Firebase Authentication (Google). We receive an account identifier and the associated email address. We never see your password.
  • Progress: your game profile (gold, purchases, scores, achievements, character settings) and the spells you forge, stored on our server and linked to your identifier.
  • Spell forge: the text you write on a scroll is sent to AI services (Anthropic, and PixelLab for images) to create the spell.
  • Multiplayer: co-op games are peer to peer (WebRTC). Your IP address is visible to the other players of the game and, if needed, to our relay server (TURN). It is not kept.

The offline game saves progress only on your device.

Use

This data is only used to run the game: logging you in, saving your progress, forging your spells and connecting you to other players. It is neither sold nor used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Account and progress data are kept as long as the account exists. You can delete them at any time from the My account page (account, progress and spells are then erased), or request their deletion by writing to not.io.dev@gmail.com; they will then be erased from our server.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing, by writing to not.io.dev@gmail.com.
