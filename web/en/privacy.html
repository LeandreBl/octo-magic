Privacy policy

Updated November 17, 2026

This page explains what data the game processes when you play online, and how to have it erased.

Data collected Use Retention and deletion Your rights

Data collected

Account
Login goes through Firebase Authentication (email and password, or Google). We receive an account identifier and the associated email address. We never see your password.
Progress
Your game profile (gold, purchases, scores, achievements, character settings) and your spells, stored on our server and tied to your identifier.
Spell creation
The text you write on a scroll is sent to our providers (Anthropic, and PixelLab for the images) to create the spell.
Multiplayer
Co-op games are peer to peer (WebRTC). Your IP address is visible to the other players in the game and, when needed, to our relay server (TURN). It is not kept.

Offline, progress is saved only on your device.

Use

This data is used only to run the game: logging you in, saving your progress, creating your spells and connecting you to other players. It is neither sold nor used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Account and progress data are kept as long as the account exists. You can delete them at any time from the My account page: account, progress and spells are then erased from our server.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can access your data, correct it, delete it or object to its processing.
