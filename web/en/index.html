Your spells

One word. A spell nobody else has.

Wrest blank scrolls from the dungeon's bosses. Inscribe a word of power on each one — in Latin, Ancient Greek, Old Norse, Sanskrit… and it comes to life: its shape, its path, its element, its effects.

Combine your scrolls and your spell transforms: the slowing frost becomes a storm that freezes everything in its path, fire starts to bounce, lightning hunts down its prey. Every combination makes a spell that exists nowhere else.
