# Sound credits

Recorded spell sounds (a continuous body's `sound`, looped while it lives) in place of the synthesized ones (SpellSound). CC0
(public domain dedication); attribution is not required but given here. Each
is a cut of the source, mono 22.05 kHz Ogg Vorbis, its tail crossfaded into
its head so it loops without a click.

## lightning.ogg
- Title: Electricity Sound Effects, continuousspark.wav (a small Tesla generator, recorded)
- Author: BMacZero
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/electricity-sound-effects-0
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
